For interested creators

Important engagement terms

Any creator relationship is governed by a binding written agreement between the creator and NovaX Studios Ltd. Unless the signed agreement states otherwise, either party must give two months' written notice to end the service. For a creator aged 16 or 17, the parent or legal guardian acting for the creator, and NovaX, may instead end the agreement with two weeks' written notice. Any royalty or management fee during a notice period remains governed by the signed agreement.

This summary is provided for general information only. It is not legal advice and does not replace the terms of a signed agreement. Prospective creators should review the full contract and may wish to obtain independent legal advice before signing.
