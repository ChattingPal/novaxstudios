Creator applications

YouTube application criteria.

Please confirm that you meet every requirement below before submitting an application to NovaX Studios™.

  1. 01

    Subscriber base

    Your YouTube channel must have at least 1,000 subscribers.

  2. 02

    Monetization

    Your YouTube channel must have active monetization.

  3. 03

    Recent activity

    Your channel must have published a video or hosted a live stream within the past 30 days. YouTube Shorts do not count toward this requirement.

  4. 04

    Age requirement

    Applicants must be at least 16 years old. Applicants aged 16 or 17 must have a parent or legal guardian co-sign the agreement.

Current platform eligibility

Only YouTube content creators may apply at this time. Other social-media accounts may be included if the application is approved. NovaX Studios™ reserves the right to decline any application that does not meet these criteria, or where there is reasonable concern that an applicant is 15 or younger or has submitted fraudulent information.
