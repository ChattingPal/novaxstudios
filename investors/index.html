Investor relations

Backing independent creators, responsibly.

NovaX Studios Ltd. is a pre-revenue private limited company based in the United Kingdom. We are building a creator-management business designed to give independent talent access to professional resources without taking control of their work.

Company stage Pre-revenue
Legal structure UK private limited company
Investment interest From €1,000

The vision

Infrastructure for creative independence.

Each creator operates independently. NovaX Studios does not dictate their content, creative direction or production decisions. Our role is to remove practical barriers and provide the resources that allow stronger ideas to be produced professionally.

We welcome investment enquiries from €1,000 upward. There is no fixed upper tier; proposals are considered according to their terms, strategic fit and ability to fund the long-term creator-support model.

How capital supports creators

Flexible resources for different production needs.

01

Project funding

Direct funding for approved projects or videos where additional resources can raise production quality.

02

Equipment access

Professional equipment may be purchased, supplied or rented according to the requirements of the work.

03

Monthly allowances

Individual creator allowances may be introduced where appropriate. Allowances vary, and new creators may not begin with one.

Investment principle

Long-term value over short-term reductions.

We seek investors who support disciplined, sustainable spending rather than reducing creator resources solely to improve short-term returns. Every cost is assessed against production needs, responsible budgeting and the wider NovaX vision. Where a level of support is reasonable, affordable and valuable to a creator's work, we intend to protect that investment.
