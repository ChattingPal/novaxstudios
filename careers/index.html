Careers at NovaX

Build the support system behind independent creativity.

NovaX Studios Ltd. is an early-stage, pre-revenue creator-management company based in the United Kingdom. We are building a thoughtful team to help independent creators access professional resources without taking control of their voice.

Early stage United Kingdom Creator-focused

What we are building

A company creators can rely on.

Our work sits behind the content: coordinating equipment, funding production, finding the right collaborators and delivering marketing that helps good work reach the right audience.

The creator remains responsible for what they make. Our team is responsible for making the professional support around that work dependable, practical and fair.

How we intend to work

Clear principles from the beginning.

01

Protect creative independence

We support creators without dictating their ideas, voice or production choices.

02

Use resources responsibly

We balance ambitious support with disciplined decisions that keep the model sustainable.

03

Communicate directly

We value clear expectations, honest feedback and dependable follow-through.

Future areas of work

Different skills, one creator-first mission.

As NovaX grows, opportunities may emerge across the operational and creative functions that support our creators.

  • Creator partnerships
  • Production coordination
  • Editing and post-production
  • Music and talent networks
  • Marketing and audience growth
  • Operations and finance

  3. 03 Practical discussion

    Role-relevant questions or work may be used to understand how you approach problems.

  4. 04 Decision and next steps

    Selected candidates will receive the proposed terms and onboarding information.
