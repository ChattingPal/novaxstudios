About NovaX Studios

Small ideas should have the chance to become serious projects.

NovaX Studios Ltd. gives emerging creators and artists practical access to the funding, equipment and professional support needed to pursue ambitious work while preserving the independence that makes their work their own.

Our mission

Build the foundation behind the talent.

A limited budget should not decide how far a strong idea can go. Our mission is to help small creators turn early concepts into larger, professionally delivered projects and enter the entertainment world with a credible foundation and a solid start.

We support the person behind the work; we do not take ownership of them or dictate what they create. Creators retain their identity, voice and creative decision-making. NovaX provides resources and expertise around their work while the creator remains responsible for the content itself.

How we provide support

Practical backing shaped around each creator.

Every creator and project is different. Support is considered individually and may combine one or more of the following arrangements.

01

Project funding

Funding for approved videos and creative projects where additional resources can materially improve ambition, production quality or audience reach.

02

Affordable equipment access

Professional equipment may be supplied or rented to creators at substantially lower rates than typical commercial hire, subject to availability and agreement.

03

Individual budgets

Where appropriate, a creator may receive an agreed project budget or ongoing allowance. The amount and availability depend on the creator and partnership.

A proportionate partnership

The royalty reflects the work we contribute.

In exchange for funding, equipment access and professional support, NovaX receives an agreed royalty or fee. The level is set according to the resources, risk and work involved in the partnership. It is not a one-size-fits-all arrangement.

The commercial terms are documented in a written agreement before support begins. Our aim is a transparent exchange that gives creators room to grow while allowing NovaX to continue funding new work responsibly.
