About NovaX Studios

Small ideas should have the chance to become serious projects.

NovaX Studios Ltd. aims to help emerging creators and artists gain practical access to funding, equipment and professional support as they pursue ambitious work, while seeking to preserve the independence that makes their work their own.

Our mission

Build the foundation behind the talent.

A limited budget should not have to decide how far a strong idea can go. Our ambition is to help small creators develop early concepts into larger, professionally presented projects and build a stronger foundation for entering the entertainment world.

Our intended approach is to support the person behind the work without taking ownership of them or directing what they create. The aim is for creators to retain their identity, voice and creative decision-making while NovaX offers resources and expertise around their work. Creators would remain responsible for the content itself.

How we aim to provide support

Practical backing intended to fit each creator.

Every creator and project is different. Any support would be considered individually and could combine one or more of the following arrangements, subject to resources, suitability and agreement.

01

Project funding

Potential funding for approved videos and creative projects where additional resources could help improve ambition, production quality or audience reach.

02

Affordable equipment access

Where suitable, professional equipment may be supplied or rented to creators at reduced rates, subject to availability, affordability and agreement.

03

Individual budgets

Where appropriate and financially sustainable, a creator could receive an agreed project budget or ongoing allowance. Any amount and availability would depend on the creator, project and partnership.

A proportionate partnership

Any royalty would reflect the work involved.

Where NovaX and a creator agree to work together, proposed support would be provided in exchange for an agreed royalty or fee. The level would be considered according to the resources, risk and work involved in the partnership rather than using a one-size-fits-all arrangement.

Commercial terms would be documented in a written agreement before any support begins. Our goal is to build a transparent exchange intended to give creators room to grow while helping NovaX work towards funding new projects responsibly.
