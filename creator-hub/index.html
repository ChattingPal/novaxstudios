What is NovaX Creator Hub?

NovaX Creator Hub is a web platform for independent creators. Its current public beta provides creator profiles, creator discovery and account tools, with additional platform features remaining under development.

Developed and operated by NovaX Studios Ltd.

NovaX Creator Hub is a digital platform developed and operated by NovaX Studios Ltd., the company behind the NovaX Studios brand. It is one of the online platforms within the company's wider digital media and technology work.

NovaX Studios Ltd. is responsible for the platform's development, operation and published service information. Creator Hub uses the NovaX name as a product of the same company rather than as a separate business.

What does it do?

Tools available during the public beta.

The current platform focuses on public creator identity, discovery and secure account management. Only features already available are described below.

Profiles

Public creator pages

Creators can publish a public handle, profile image and description, then manage that profile from their account.

Discovery

Creator search

Visitors can browse public profiles and search creators by handle, with verified creators displayed first.

Accounts

Secure account access

Email and password authentication, Google and Discord sign-in, password recovery and account management are available.

Current development status

Public profiles now. More features later.

Creator profiles, profile editing and the searchable creator directory are available in beta. News feeds, community forums, merchandise features and paid subscriptions are not currently available for purchase or general use.

Planned features may change during development. Their appearance in platform previews or service documents does not mean they have launched.
